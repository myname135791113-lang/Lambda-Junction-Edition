# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Junction hackathon judges and partners watching the team's demo, on a laptop or a projector, and
reading the shared report link afterwards.

## Product Purpose
Lambda maps an object by touch with a Rokoko Smartglove: from 30 to 60 seconds of handling it works out
shape (box, cylinder or sphere), dimensions, material, mass and friction, writes an object card (JSON),
and a robot hand uses that card to grasp the same kind of object with the right force. The visual test
report is the proof: it shows the simulated runs, their numbers and their failures.

## Positioning
Gloves only: no force sensing in the glove and no Coil Pro. Cheap FSR pads plus a joint fit across
grasps recover the object, and the tap test identifies material so the robot neither drops nor crushes it.

## Operating Context
The report is generated by `python -m glove_twin.visual` into `reports/<name>/index.html`, with GIFs,
cards and twin files next to it. Shown live during the hackathon demo and shared as a link.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- The report's data, copy, charts and GIFs are generated by the simulation and must not be altered by
  visual work.
- Light and dark themes, with a toggle.
- Must read well on a projector: large, high-contrast numbers and charts.
- Self-contained: self-hosted fonts, no CDN at runtime.

## Evidence on Hand
Simulated results only (seeded runs). Ground-truth object data with sources lives in
`glove_twin/objects.py` and the README. No real-hardware results yet; never present simulated numbers
as measured ones.

## Product Principles
- Honest numbers: unmeasured dimensions say "not measured", failures (dropped, crushed) are shown plainly.
- Simulated is labelled as simulated.
- Show the mechanism working, not claims about it.
